BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A little clarity
goes a long way.

Do I need to be an experienced lifter?

No. Your starting point helps determine the plan. New and returning lifters can focus on foundational technique, clear instruction, and consistent training.

Personal coaching or a training program?

Personalized coaching fits your circumstances and provides feedback and adjustments. A ready-made program suits someone comfortable training independently. Use the consultation to discuss current options and pricing.

What if my schedule or equipment is limited?

Tell me what you can realistically commit to and what equipment you have. Those constraints help shape your plan.

What happens after I reach out?

The consultation form is a place to share goals and ask questions. We can discuss your experience, needs, and whether coaching is a good fit before you decide to move forward.
