I am a former college athlete, strength and conditioning coach, and strongman competitor. I have spent years training, competing, and helping athletes, strength competitors, and everyday lifters improve their physical performance.

I help people become faster, stronger, and more capable through purposeful strength and conditioning. Personalized coaching begins with your specific goals, training experience, available equipment, weekly schedule, current ability, and any limitations that affect how you train. Those details are used to create a clear plan with appropriate exercises, training volume, intensity, and progression—not a one-size-fits-all routine.

You will understand what to do in each workout, why each exercise is included, and how your performance should progress over time. Practical coaching and honest feedback help you improve technique, manage recovery, work through plateaus, and make adjustments when your results or circumstances change. Progress is measured through relevant indicators such as improved movement quality, faster performance, heavier successful lifts, greater work capacity, and more consistent training.

If you are willing to work consistently, I will provide the structure and direction needed to pursue real, measurable progress. Use the consultation form to discuss your goals and determine whether personalized coaching is the right service for you. If you prefer to train independently, you can instead choose a proven training program built around one of the goal categories described below.

Why Hire Me as Your Strength Coach?

  • Individualized planning: Training is built around your goals, current ability, experience, schedule, available equipment, and relevant limitations.
  • Clear exercise instruction: Learn sound technique, useful movement cues, and the purpose of each exercise so you can train with confidence.
  • Structured progression: Workouts organize exercise selection, sets, repetitions, intensity, and recovery so improvement can be measured over time.
  • Ongoing adjustments: Receive honest feedback and practical changes when performance, recovery, scheduling, or training needs shift.
  • Goal-specific development: Build the strength, speed, power, work capacity, or general fitness that matters for your chosen outcome.
  • Accountability: Stay consistent through focused expectations, encouragement, and a plan that makes the next training step clear.
  • Sustainable training: Train hard while respecting movement quality, fatigue management, recovery, and long-term development.
  • Plateau support: Identify what is limiting progress and adjust technique, workload, exercise selection, or recovery instead of guessing.

Choose Your Training Program

Athletic Training
For athletes who want physical preparation that transfers to their sport. Develop acceleration, speed, explosive power, total-body strength, coordination, and movement quality while balancing training demands with practices and competition.
Strongman Training
For new or experienced strongman competitors who need event-focused preparation. Build total-body strength, grip, bracing, conditioning, and efficient technique for demanding carries, overhead presses, pulls, loading events, and other competition movements.
Powerlifting
For lifters focused on increasing their squat, bench press, and deadlift. Improve competition-lift technique, build supporting muscle and strength, manage training intensity, and follow measurable progression toward stronger lifts or meet preparation.
General Fitness
For adults who want effective training without preparing for a specific sport or competition. Improve foundational strength, movement quality, work capacity, and confidence through practical workouts that support health, recreation, and the physical demands of daily life.

Why Use a Training Program?

A structured program tells you which exercises to perform and organizes the sets, repetitions, intensity, and progression of your workouts. This gives every session a purpose, provides a consistent way to track performance, and removes the guesswork of deciding what to train each day.

Following a plan also helps balance productive challenge with technique practice and recovery. Instead of changing workouts at random, you can evaluate results over time and determine whether the program is moving you toward your goal. A ready-made program is best for someone comfortable training independently; personalized coaching is the better choice when you need a plan tailored to your circumstances, direct feedback, accountability, or ongoing adjustments.
