# Daily operation and weekly review

Planned business block: approximately 3–5 p.m. daily, America/New_York. Fourteen planned hours weekly is capacity, not observed work or eligible course time. Keep IT 195, IT 406, and commercial delivery records separate and never count overlapping time twice.

| Time | Default concrete output |
| --- | --- |
| 3:00–3:10 | Review private inquiries and pick one achievable output. |
| 3:10–4:00 | Record footage, write a carousel, or prepare a cleared asset. |
| 4:00–4:25 | Edit, subtitle, review factual claims, and prepare the final draft. |
| 4:25–4:45 | Peter handles conversations; otherwise improve the inquiry journey. |
| 4:45–5:00 | Record actual outcomes and leave tomorrow's first action. |

Start with two footage posts and one written/carousel post per week. Use remaining blocks for batching, inquiry handling, and improvements. This is an experiment; adjust from actual response and workload. Publication dates are Peter's decision. Reserve specific delivery blocks before accepting the first client; content and delivery cannot both consume the same capacity.

## Weekly measurement definitions

Keep identifiable lead/client rows outside Git. Use an anonymous weekly aggregate here only after actual observations; absent analytics means unknown, not zero. Pick a consistent week start/end and timezone. Record source and observation date for each aggregate.

| Metric | Definition | Initial actual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Published pieces | Distinct posts actually published during the week; drafts excluded. | Unknown |
| Qualified coaching inquiries | Distinct people requesting beginner coaching who provide enough context for Peter to assess fit; duplicate messages counted once. | Unknown |
| Conversations | Distinct inquiry contacts with a substantive two-way discussion during the week. | Unknown |
| Quotes | Written scopes with an approved price actually sent by Peter. | Unknown |
| Clients | Distinct people with an agreed coaching engagement; distinguish new clients from active clients. | Unknown |
| Cash received | Payments actually received, with currency and recording basis; proposed prices excluded. | Unknown |
| Refunds | Refunds actually processed, with currency; report separately from cash received. | Unknown |
| Delivery/support time | Peter-confirmed actual effort, classified by delivery, check-ins, administration, and support. | Unknown |

Weekly review in the final available block:

1. Compare outputs and observed progression: published piece → inquiry → conversation → quote → client. If traffic/exposure is unknown, do not calculate a visitor conversion rate.
2. Look for one observed stopping point. Ask whether the obstacle is unclear CTA, unsuitable audience, missing offer terms, or unallocated delivery capacity. Separate evidence from hypotheses.
3. Choose one change for the next batch, write why it may help, and preserve everything else practical for comparison. Small samples support learning, not claims of proven marketing effectiveness.
4. Confirm next week's capacity and outstanding promised deliveries before considering another client.

Weekly aggregate template: dates/timezone; source and completeness; the eight actual metrics above; observed questions; one hypothesis; one next change; Peter-confirmed hours if supplied; next action. Empty/unknown entries stay explicit. Never infer hours from commits, agent runtime, or these planned blocks.
