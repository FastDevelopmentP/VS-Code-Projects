Fulfillment sequence:

1. Peter reviews the inquiry for fit and clarifies goals, experience, equipment, schedule, and expectations. Obtain only information necessary for the service; do not collect detailed private health information through public comments or this repository.
2. Prepare a written scope using the approved fields; Peter sends it. Inquiry is not enrollment.
3. Before agreement/payment, confirm the recipient understood deliverables, limits, price, dates, and cancellation/refund terms. Peter handles commitments and payment through the separately approved method.
4. Reserve delivery capacity. Peter prepares and reviews the initial program, delivers it through the approved channel, and confirms receipt.
5. Hold four scheduled check-ins. Record completion, requested changes, delivery/support time, and the next agreed action privately. Do not infer results from attendance alone.
6. At the end, review the client's reported experience, actual delivery effort, and any unfulfilled commitments. Renewal is a separate decision, never automatic.

Pilot review: compare actual time with reserved capacity; confirm all promised deliveries occurred; review unresolved questions and cancellations/refunds. Expand client capacity only after Peter allocates time and approves the revised offer.
