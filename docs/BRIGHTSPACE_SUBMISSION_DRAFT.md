## A. Project-specific design decisions

1. The Products & Services page uses four matching cards for software, programs, consulting, and one-on-one coaching. Repeated headings (who it is for, what is included, next step) help visitors compare each option.
2. The new page uses the landing page's charcoal, cream, and lime colors, condensed headings, buttons, header, and footer. These choices make the two pages feel like one FAST Strength website.
3. Software and programs carry clear “In development” labels and use existing generated concept images. This explains their current status without suggesting that the illustrations show a released product or that purchases are available.

## B. Common website conventions

1. Both pages use header navigation with Home and Products & Services links. An underlined active link and aria-current identify the current page; visitors can return home easily.
2. Responsive cards sit in two columns on desktop and stack on phones. The navigation wraps on smaller screens, keeping page links available without sideways scrolling.
3. Descriptive contact buttons appear within the offering cards and a consultation button closes the page. This gives visitors a next step near the information they are considering.
